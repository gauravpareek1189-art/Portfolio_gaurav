A LITTLE BIT ABOUT ME

I like making things that feel impossible, tactile and
a little unfamiliar.

My practice moves between graphic design, art direction and 3D. I'm interested in the point where a flat idea starts feeling physical — and where a physical object starts behaving like an image.
